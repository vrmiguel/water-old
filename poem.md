# Ode to Water

In lines of text where modules flow,
A tiny compiler learns to grow.
From wat to wasm, clear and tight,
It shapes the bytes to run just right.

Sections stream like quiet rain,
Opcodes hum inside the brain.
Parsers sip from every token,
Proof that specs need not be broken.

In rusted strength, the engine gleams,
A lean companion for your dreams.
Small in scope, but sharp and sure—
`water` makes your wasm pure.

So let the larger tools compete,
With features vast and heavy feet;
This subtle stream, precise and light,
Will carry code to distant byte.
