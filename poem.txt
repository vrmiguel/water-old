The Weaver of Wasm

In silicon fields where logic grows,
A weaver sits and weaves in rows,
Not cloth of thread, but bytes of light,
Transforming text to code at night.

From human words to structured streams,
The parser traces syntax dreams,
Each token placed with careful hand,
Instructions bloom across the land.

The stack machine begins to hum,
As integers and floats become
A language bridging mind and chip,
A compiled, calculated trip.

So raise a glass to code well spun,
To compilers built and benchmarks run,
For in each opcode, small yet grand,
A poem the machine can understand.
