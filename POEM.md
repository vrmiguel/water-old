# A Compiler's Tale

In silicon dreams and binary streams,
Where abstract syntax trees take flight,
We parse the code, decode the schemes,
Transform the source to bytes of light.

Through lexer's gaze and parser's mind,
Each token finds its rightful place,
Instructions woven, intertwined,
In WebAssembly's embrace.

From Rust we craft with care and pride,
A compiler strong and true,
Where opcodes flow like evening tide,
And modules spring up, fresh and new.

The LEB128 numbers dance,
Compressed within their clever form,
While emitters seize their chance,
To shape the bytecode's perfect norm.

So let us build with precision fine,
Each function, import, constant clear,
A testament to our design,
A compiler held most dear.
