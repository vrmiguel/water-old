# Water, Compiled

In brackets of breath, a program learns to flow—  
glyphs of WAT, stacked stones in a clear stream.  
We name each current: `func`, `param`, `result`,  
and the river keeps its promise, small and keen.

The bytes arrive like rain on warm tin roofs,  
rhythm in hex, percussion in the dark.  
A module closes, tight as cupped hands,  
holding one bright drop: an executable spark.

Between text and tide, the compiler listens—  
swift, spare, exact.  
What’s written becomes water,  
and what is water runs.
