# Water, In Text and Byte

In brackets, a promise: `(module)` begins,
a shoreline of symbols where meaning lives.
One keyword, one ripple, one careful name,
until the stack remembers why it came.

We pour in `i32`, we pour in `i64`,
thin streams of intent through a narrow door.
The parser listens, patient and strict,
turning whitespace into something it can fix.

Then silence—compiled—becomes a tide,
byte after byte in measured stride.
Not loud, not large, just clean and tight:
water to wasm, text into light.
