## Water, in Text

I write the river in parentheses,
glyphs that hold a current still—
`(module)` cupping quiet seas,
and every byte obeying will.

The source is mist: a spoken form,
names and numbers, breath and sign;
I parse it into patient norm,
then pour it into strict design.

Opcodes peck like winter birds,
constants sink, then rise in foam;
imports knock in borrowed words,
functions fold the world back home.

So let the wat in letters run,
let braces make a shore of trust;
when text is done and bits are spun,
the stream compiles—bright, small, and just.
